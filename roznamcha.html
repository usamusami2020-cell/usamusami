Roznamcha: my daily diary

By Usman bin Affan Khan · usamusami.com

Roznamcha is a word for a daily diary, a record of days as they pass. This page is where I keep mine.

Here I write my own days, entry by entry and date by date: what I studied and what I am researching, where I travelled, the people I met, the thoughts that stayed with me, and the small lessons an ordinary day can carry. Some entries will be short, some longer. Some will be about study, some about journeys, some about a quiet day that taught more than a busy one.

This is not teaching and not advice. It is one person's days, written honestly, so that the days are not lost, and so that anyone who reads may find a companion in them.

Entries will appear here by date, newest first. The first entry is on its way.
