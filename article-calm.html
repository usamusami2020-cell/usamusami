Reflection

A restless heart, and the way back to calm

By Usman bin Affan Khan · usamusami.com

Some days the heart feels heavy for no clear reason. Thoughts keep circling, sleep feels light, and even good news does not lift the mood. This restlessness is a normal part of being human. No one stays in one state forever, and a heavy day is not a failed day.

The Qur'an speaks to this state directly and gently: “Verily, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest” (Surah Ar-Ra'd, 13:28). Calm, in this teaching, is not an escape from life. It is a return: the heart comes back to the One who made it, and settles.

There are small, practical doors back to calm. Sit quietly for a few minutes and remember Allah with a calm tongue and a present heart. Make dua in your own simple words, naming exactly what is weighing on you. Slow down the inputs for a while: less news, less scrolling, more silence. Take a short walk and let the body move. Finish one small task completely, and let the mind taste order again.

It also helps to remember that feelings move. The heaviness of this morning is not a promise about tonight. Treat your own heart the way you would treat a tired friend: with patience, not with scolding.

And if a heavy state stays for a long time and begins to disturb daily life, there is no shame in speaking to someone you trust and seeking proper help. Faith and effort walk together. The way back to calm is usually not one big step, but many small, honest ones.

This writing is educational only. It is not a diagnosis or a treatment, and it never replaces a qualified professional.
