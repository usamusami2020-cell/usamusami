About me

About me

I am Usman bin Affan Khan, known online as UsamUsami. I am a student and researcher of Tafsir, and a writer.

I was born in Saudi Arabia and spent my early years there, before moving to Bangladesh for my Islamic education. I am currently based in Karachi, Pakistan, where I am completing a specialisation in Tafsir and its sciences at Jamia Binoria Alamia.

My writing centres on three themes: the Qur'an, the human mind, and mental wellbeing. I work to present reliable psychological knowledge in simple language, held together with an Islamic perspective, so that readers can understand their own minds and face the pressures of everyday life with greater calm.

My writing appears regularly on my blog, and this website is the permanent home of my work.
